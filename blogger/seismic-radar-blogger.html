Market pulse

Exploration is recovering at two speeds. Real activity arrives late 2026 and, above all, in 2027.

Read from the Q2 results of TGS, Shearwater and Viridien and from what their CEOs said at ROG.e and the Pareto conference: more talk and more tendering, but spending has not taken off yet.

Pace
TGS is running near full (94% streamer utilisation, USD 756M backlog) on the back of multi-client. Shearwater had a weak contract year and expects a similar Q3. Viridien's revenue is down, but its Geoscience backlog is up 19%.
Timing
Backlogs point to an upturn towards year-end and into 2027: Shearwater at USD 401M with visibility into 2027, and TGS's CEO expects exploration budgets to rise next year.
Where
Brazil (Equatorial Margin, Santos, Pelotas), West Africa (Angola, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire), India, Guyana, and the North Sea and Norway. The Middle East is slowed by the conflict.
Pricing
Streamer contract prices remain flat. TGS reports losing tenders to bids 15–20% lower: more work, but contractors are still guarding margins.
Crews
Offshore hiring (QC/HSE, client reps, marine crews) usually follows awards by a few months. The likely window is Q4 2026 to Q1 2027, led by Brazil, West Africa, India and the North Sea.
