Seismic Radar is an independent, regularly updated page for people who work in or around marine seismic: crews, QC/HSE and client representatives, vessel operators, recruiters and anyone following offshore exploration.

It brings together three things that are usually scattered across press releases, trade media and investor presentations:

Market pulse: a plain-language read of activity levels, backlog, pricing and regional hotspots, and what that means for offshore hiring.
News feed: contracts, multi-client surveys, results, technology and licensing news, each summarised with a link to the original source.
Dates to watch: the conferences and reporting dates that shape the next read.

Summaries are written from public sources. The market pulse is an editorial reading, not investment advice
